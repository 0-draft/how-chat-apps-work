The simple case: A and B both have the app open. Two ideas carry the whole chapter. Order comes
from a `seq` number [the server assigns](#overview-3), never from anyone's clock. And
"delivered" and "read" are sent as [a position](#overview-8), "everything up to here", not once
per message.
