Chat apps differ in the details, but the cast is almost always the same. A **gateway** holds an
always-on connection to every phone. A **messaging service** puts messages in order and stores
them. The **OS push service** wakes phones that aren't connected. And an **object store** holds
anything too big to send inline.

Every diagram on this page uses a made-up protocol. Real apps name their frames and fields
differently, but they are solving the same problems. How specific apps do it is in
[Real apps](#apps).
