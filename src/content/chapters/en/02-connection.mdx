title: The always-on connection
short: Always-on connection

---

A web page only answers when asked, so a server has no way to push a message down to it. Chat
apps work around that by opening one connection at launch and keeping it until the app goes
away. Every round trip spent setting it up is time you spend staring at stale messages.

Discord documents its version of this openly: the server's first frame (Hello, opcode 10) tells
the client [how often to heartbeat](#connection-12), and a client that drops can resume and have
the events it missed replayed.
