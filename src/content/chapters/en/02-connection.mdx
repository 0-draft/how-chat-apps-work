title: The always-on connection
short: Always-on connection

---

Plain HTTP only lets the server speak when it's asked, so it has no way to push a message down to
the phone. Chat apps work around that by opening one connection at launch and keeping it until the app goes
away. Every round trip spent setting it up is time you spend staring at stale messages.

Discord documents its version: the server's first frame (Hello, opcode 10) tells
the client [how often to heartbeat](#connection-12), and a client that drops can resume and have
the events it missed replayed.
